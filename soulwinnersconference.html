Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about attending Soul Winners Conference 2026.

Is Soul Winners Conference a paid event?

No. Soul Winners Conference is 100% free to attend. Registration is compulsory to reserve your seat and digital delegate pass.

Is feeding provided during the conference?

Delegates handle their own meals. Numerous restaurants and quality food vendors operate right around Adamasingba Stadium.

Can I invite friends, workers, or my whole ministry team?

Yes! Cell groups, youth fellowships, and evangelism teams are welcome. Each delegate should register individually for their pass.
